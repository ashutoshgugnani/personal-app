ABS PROTOCOL - install on your iPhone (5 minutes, free)

The app is 100% local: all data (workouts, weight, calories, streaks)
is stored on your phone only. Works offline after the first load.

STEP 1 - Put it online (one time, free, no account needed):
  Option A - Netlify Drop (easiest):
    1. On any computer or your phone, go to https://app.netlify.com/drop
    2. Drag/upload this whole folder (all files together)
    3. You get a link like https://something.netlify.app
  Option B - GitHub Pages:
    1. Create a repo, upload these files, enable Pages in Settings
    2. Your link: https://yourname.github.io/reponame

STEP 2 - Install on your iPhone:
    1. Open the link in SAFARI (must be Safari, not Chrome)
    2. Tap the Share button (square with arrow)
    3. Tap "Add to Home Screen"
    4. Tap "Add"

Done. The Abs Protocol icon is now on your home screen.
It opens full-screen like a native app and works offline.

NOTE: Your data lives in that app's local storage. Don't delete
the app icon without noting your stats, and avoid "Clear History
and Website Data" in Safari settings, which wipes it.
